In the detail

One card, under the light

This is a card from The Academy, rendered live in a single WebGL shader — a crystal frame with cut facets and gold trim, a parchment face rasterised to a texture, and a foil layer that answers the light.

The foil isn't random sparkle. Hotspots are pinned to the frame's natural flare points — corners rake with streak-crosses, edge-midpoints bloom with diffuse spectra — and each one flashes white-hot only when the moving light truly aligns with it, the way a real foil card behaves in your hand.

Details matter. This one took an evening of sliders, argument, and gold.
